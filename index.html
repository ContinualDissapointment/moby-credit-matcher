Step 1 — grab the credits

MobyGames sits behind Cloudflare, so this page can’t download credits for you. Instead, your own browser does it:

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Grab Moby Credits
  2. Open a MobyGames credits page, e.g. /game/134188/catherine-full-body/credits/playstation-4/
  3. Click the bookmark. The credits are copied to your clipboard.
  4. Paste below. Repeat for the second game.
